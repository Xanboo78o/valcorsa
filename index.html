SETTINGS

Steering

Zones = tap-hold the arrows (bottom-left). Halves = the whole screen: press a side to turn that way. Tilt = turn the phone like a wheel.

Throttle

Auto = always on the gas, hold BRAKE to slow. Lean = tip the phone toward flat to accelerate, pull it back to brake. Pedals = full control: GAS, BRAKE & DRIFT strips.

Soundtrack

My Music mutes the Valcorsa radio and steps aside so whatever you're playing — Spotify, an album, anything — keeps going. Engine and effects stay.

The radio is off. Start your own music, then come race — on some phones you may need to restart the song once after the game loads.
