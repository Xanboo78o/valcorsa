## HERO 1 — CIRCUITO DREI PUENTES · "The Show" (25 Q, LOCKED)

**The capital of Valcorsa is DREI PUENTES** — not San Volante (canon bomb, Q2/Q3).
Three bridges; a Germanic+Spanish fused name — the city where the settler tongues met.

- **Identity:** THE SHOW — built for spectacle, camera-bait, the clip factory. TV first.
- **Native mode:** SMASHKART (flagship mode, flagship venue). **Season role: OPENING
  NIGHT** — every season starts here; San Volante ends what Puentes begins.
- **The lap** (start/finish →): **LA VOLADORA** — monster jump OVER the main
  grandstand → **THE NEON CANYON** — walled downtown street, BOTH walls rideable,
  echo chamber, THE LOUDEST PLACE on the lap → **THE THREE BRIDGES** — flowing river
  crossings (all three, the city's name in asphalt) → **EL COLISEO DE DREI PUENTES**
  — purpose-built kart colosseum; the lap FORKS INSIDE THE BOWL (upper deck vs lower
  floor, two crowds, rejoin at the exit tunnel) → finish.
- **THE ROTATING FORK (new mechanic):** the two Coliseo branches cycle surfaces per
  lap — L1 asphalt+dirt · L2 dirt+wet · L3 wet+asphalt. Same choice, never the same
  choice twice.
- **Race format:** 3 laps. **World record 2:25.00 — Henrik "Der Uhrmacher" Voss**
  (the Watchmaker), a Heiligen master who set it and retired home to the mountains.
  The capital still holds the grudge. His ghost runs on anniversary nights.
- **Night always** — floodlit to full day visibility. **Hard League: the lights go
  DOWN** — true night, headlights matter, the canyon becomes the only bright place.
- **Fully walled, wide track.**
- **Item card:** core six + Los Monos, Pocket Storm, Scrap Magnet, and the new
  **EL PUENTE** (Adam's invention, Q17): puts you on a personal bridge above the
  track — free of obstacles and racers, your private highway for 15 seconds.
  Super rare; only spawns for P8 and lower. In the city of bridges.
- **Corners:** mixed tongues by district (Spanish river side, Germanic old town) —
  Claude drafts, Adam vetoes.
- **Minimap:** no forced silhouette — the lap is a lap.
- **Official/street name:** "Circuito Drei Puentes" · everyone calls it PUENTES.
