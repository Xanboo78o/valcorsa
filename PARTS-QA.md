19 questions over 5 rounds. Canon distilled into DESIGN.md §6.6.

## Round 1 — foundation

**Q1. How real is "actual terminology"?**
→ *Adam (own words):* "real things from the Valcorsa world, L1, means Large 1, m2,
means medium 2. like the acronyms and stuff MEAN things." — Valcorsa-world real:
decodable in-world nomenclature, not Earth anatomy verbatim.

**Q2. Rename the 190 parts or rebuild the catalog around real car anatomy?**
→ Full rebuild, sim-ready. The catalog IS the future Engineer Garage's parts list.

**Q3. Spec depth?** → Full spec sheets — numbers that mean things; VCRA can regulate
against them.

**Q4. Part pictures?**
→ *Adam's reveal:* "the garage garage, is going to be my real house garage, all the
bins will have things you can put inside, a 3d rendered off of my photos (like 30
smth photos)." — The Engineer Garage environment = his real garage, photo-scanned;
his real bins = in-game storage.

## Round 2 — the grammar

**Q5. One universal code grammar or per-family?** → Per-family, like real industry.
Each family = its own little language to get famous for knowing.

**Q6. Do codes enforce fitment?** → Yes — sizes are physical law. L doesn't fit S.
Cursed adapters can exist as parts.

**Q7. How atomic?** → FULLY ATOMIC from day one. Engines are built from blocks,
pistons, cams, heads.

**Q8. Where do brands sit?** → Brand + model + code ("Enginos Tempesta — B-L2 2400").
Brand carries personality, the code carries the truth.

## Round 3 — physics, units, history

**Q9. What ships to build with, pre-sim-core?** → THE WORKBENCH — first sim slice.
The Engineer door finally opens.

**Q10. How real is the combination math?** → Real-logic math: simplified but honest
physics, predictable to someone who learns it.

**Q11. Units?** → Real metric (cc/mm/kg) + vp "valc-power" (national dyno unit) +
VM bolt standard.

**Q12. What happens to the old 190 parts?** → Legacy collectibles. VCRA
Standardization Act retires them; owned ones become "pre-standard era" shelf relics
+ ₡ refund. The slop era becomes in-world history.

## Round 4 — the bench, identity, scale, store

**Q13. What does building feel like?**
→ *Adam (own words):* "you have to go grab all yo stuff then fill your 6 bins on the
bench with the stuff you just went to grab from the 3d garage." — The fetch-and-stage
loop: walk the garage, grab, stage the SIX BENCH BINS, build from the bins.

**Q14. Does a finished build have identity?** → Named + signed + earns its computed
designation ("WIDOWMAKER · L24T · 486 vp · built by Adam · S1").

**Q15. vp scale?** → 40–800, open top. Lawnmower ~40-80, club ~150-250, serious
~400-500, El Santo-tier ~700+, cursed builds beyond. VCRA caps races in vp.

**Q16. Shop recast?** → One big parts store, aisles by system, brands are shelves
within aisles. Packs stay generic gambling. (Sponsor-specialization was offered and
declined.)

## Round 5 — movement, bins, first slice

**Q17. Moving through garage v1?** → Tap between stations (camera glides). Station
layout mirrors the future scanned garage so the swap is seamless.

**Q18. Bin rules?** → Size-weighted: one code per bin; L fills a bin ×1, M stacks ×4,
hardware ×24. Size law taught again at the bench.

**Q19. First slice?** → ENGINE BENCH ONLY: garage stations + six bins + bench with
just the engine stack atomic. Rest of the car waits for the next slice.
